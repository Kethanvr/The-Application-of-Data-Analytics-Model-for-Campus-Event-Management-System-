Event Lifecycle Management

Full event workflow from proposal → HOD approval → Principal approval → student registration. Every step is tracked.

Real-Time Analytics

Interactive dashboards with department-level KPIs, budget breakdown, and event statistics — powered by Chart.js.

AI-Powered Assessments

Coordinators create MCQ and numerical quizzes. AI grades numerical answers and computes scores automatically.

Digital Certificates

Auto-generated PDF certificates with QR codes for every student who attends an approved event. Downloadable anytime.

Role-Based Access

Four distinct roles with carefully scoped permissions: Student, Event Coordinator, HOD, and Principal.

CSRF protection, session timeouts, and environment-aware settings. Production-ready with Supabase PostgreSQL.
